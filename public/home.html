Aaj Ka Kaam

Subah app kholo — aaj kin kin ki qist hai, aaj kitna paisa aana hai, is mahine kitna aa gaya: sab samne.

⏰

Late List

Kon kitne din late hai, khud hisaab lag jata hai. 30 din se zyada walon par lal nishan. Har number ke sath Call ka button.

📸

Photo saboot

Cheez dete waqt customer ki photo aur cheez ki photo laga do. Baad me koi behes nahi — pehchan pakki.

🗓️

Qiston ka plan

Kul qeemat aur advance likho — har qist, har tareekh ka plan khud ban jata hai. Jama, Late, Baqi — halat har qist par nazar aati hai.

🧾

Mahine ka hisaab — PDF

Poori dukaan ki report ek PDF me: kis ne diya, kis ka baqi hai. Print karke file me bhi rakh sakte hain.
