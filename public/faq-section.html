Qist Wasooli kya hai?

Ye qiston par cheezein bechne wali dukaanon ke liye hisaab ka online system hai. Customer ka record, qist plan, late list, photo saboot aur mahine ka poora hisaab — sab ek jagah, asaan Roman Urdu me.

Kya mera data mehfooz rahe ga?

Jee haan. Aap ka data mehfooz online database me save hota hai — phone kho jaye ya kharab ho jaye, hisaab mehfooz rehta hai. Har dukaan ka data alag hota hai, koi doosri dukaan use nahi dekh sakti.

Kaun si dukaanain istemal kar sakti hain?

Mobile shops, electronics, furniture, bike — aur har wo dukaan jo qiston par cheezein bechti hai.
